Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Learn how information is processed on Dalkhola Online.

1. Data Collection & Analytics

Dalkhola Online operates as a static website hosted on Cloud. We do not maintain user databases, require registration, or collect personal identifying information (PII) from casual visitors browsing our city directory. Data collection is only required for specific services with users consent.

2. Client-Side Features

Features like live environment updates (weather, air quality, local time) or search indexing process requests in your local browser using client-side scripts. No personal user data is sent back to our servers or stored permanently.

3. Third-Party Services & Cookies

Our pages may load assets from third-party networks to provide rich experiences (such as Google Fonts, Cloudinary for optimized media delivery, and embedded Google Maps for location guidance). These external services may collect standard web logs (IP addresses, user agents) in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

4. Open Data & Automated Workflows

Our city datasets are updated transparently using GitHub Actions and automated Python pipelines. Public directory information (e.g., business names, public office contact numbers) is sourced from public records or voluntary listings.

5. Contacting Us

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy or wish to request an update/correction to a business or public service listing, please email us at admin@dalkhola.online

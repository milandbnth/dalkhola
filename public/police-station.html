About Dalkhola Police Station

Dalkhola Police Station is a police station of the West Bengal Police serving Dalkhola and its surrounding area in the Islampur Police District of West Bengal.

Dalkhola is an important commercial and transit centre of North Bengal, situated along major road and railway routes near the border with Bihar. The police station functions under the Sub-Divisional Police Officer (SDPO), Dalkhola, and the Superintendent of Police, Islampur Police District.

Police Responsibilities

Police stations perform essential law-and-order and public safety functions. The following are general responsibilities of a police station in West Bengal; the exact procedure for each matter should be confirmed at the station.

  • Registration and investigation of criminal cases.
  • Maintenance of law and order in the jurisdiction.
  • Prevention and detection of crime.
  • Patrolling and local beat policing.
  • Traffic regulation and road safety support.
  • Assistance to women, children and senior citizens in distress.
  • Community policing and liaison with local residents.
  • Crowd management at festivals, markets and public events.
  • Verification-related police work and no-objection reports.

Public Services & Complaints

Common public dealings with a police station include the following. Availability, documents required, fees and timelines should be confirmed with the station or the police district office.

  • Filing an FIR or a written complaint.
  • General diary (GD) entry for lost documents or articles.
  • Copies of reports and case-related documents.
  • Assistance in missing-person and missing-article matters.
  • Complaints relating to domestic disputes and harassment.
  • Character and antecedent verification requests.
  • Reporting cyber fraud and financial fraud (also call 1930).

A written complaint can be submitted at the police station counter. If a station declines to register an FIR in a cognizable matter, the complaint can be taken up with the SDPO, Dalkhola, and then with the Superintendent of Police, Islampur Police District — 03526-256008, spislampurpd@gmail.com.

About Dalkhola Municipality

Dalkhola Municipality is the urban local government body responsible for civic administration and urban development in Dalkhola, Uttar Dinajpur district, West Bengal.

Dalkhola is an important commercial and transit centre of North Bengal. Its location along major road and railway routes supports trade, transport, markets and regional economic activity.

Municipal Responsibilities

Municipalities perform essential urban civic functions. The following are relevant municipal service areas; individual application procedures and availability should be confirmed with the municipality.

  • Urban roads and local infrastructure.
  • Drainage and stormwater management.
  • Water supply coordination and infrastructure.
  • Solid waste management and sanitation.
  • Street lighting coordination.
  • Building plan and local development matters.
  • Urban planning and civic improvement.
  • Public health and sanitation initiatives.
  • Support for government welfare programmes.

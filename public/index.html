Getting here

Dalkhola is a major arterial junction, making it exceptionally accessible by both road and rail networks.

  • By railDalkhola Railway Station (DLK) connects directly across Katihar Division lines.
  • By roadNational Highways NH 12 and NH 27 connect the city smoothly across state borders.
  • By airBagdogra Airport serves as the nearest primary flight transit gateway.
